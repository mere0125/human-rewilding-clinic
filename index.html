About this Clinic

In this near-future setting, a community health pilot offers consultations about people's relationships with their everyday environments. A visit moves from a concern to an assessment, a proposed change and a review of who can carry it out.

Environmental Variability is the working department in this demo. Microbial and Metabolic Ecologies and Ecological Reciprocity describe the wider institution; their services are still being developed.

Study notes

Room 001 is a constructed case. The model predicts temperature under stated assumptions. The Clinic adds authored rules for interpreting that record.

Record and sources

Imports stay in your browser. Room geometry and thermal parameters remain those of this case.

Temperature file format

Include timestamp, outdoor_temp_c and indoor_temp_c. Timestamps need an explicit time-zone offset. Optional columns are hvac_fraction or hvac_on, and window_open. Blank optional channels remain unknown. Duplicate timestamps and missing paired temperatures stop the import.

Gaps longer than 1.5 times the typical interval break the trace. The model restarts from the next recorded indoor temperature. Importing a file does not calibrate the model.

Temperature calculation

The model balances heat through the envelope, outdoor air exchange, internal gains and heating or cooling. It updates one average indoor temperature in steps of at most sixty seconds.

C Ṫi = H(To − Ti) + ρ cp q(To − Ti) + Qg + Qh

H describes envelope conductance. C is effective heat capacity. q is outdoor airflow. Internal and conditioning power enter as heat flows. Air changes per hour are an explicit assumption.

The Clinic architectural study

The Clinic contains consultation, environmental trial and operations spaces. Portal travel, louvers and a replaceable mesh cassette are movable architectural mechanisms. Their environmental performance has not been calculated. The separate Room 001 model remains the only thermal calculation.

The patient may consent, pause or end the local rehearsal. Staff can revise the artwork’s classification rules with a visible reason and version history. The perspective switch is a demonstration, not authentication. Session changes stay in page memory and clear on reload.

What the spatial view shows

The window and room logger follow the selected record interval. The room has one calculated temperature. Daylight follows the display clock for orientation, while the heat model excludes solar gain. Window angle represents an operating state and does not determine ventilation. The surrounding buildings and courtyard provide a constructed local setting.

Interval heat ledger

The ledger integrates envelope exchange, ventilation, internal gains and signed conditioning over each model interval. Their sum equals the change in stored heat, C(Tnext − Tnow). It displays each integrated energy divided by the interval duration as a mean power in W. A positive value enters the room.

These are terms in the existing room heat balance. They are not a spatial airflow calculation or a measured thermal image. A temperature-only import cannot supply them; a selected modelled operation can.

Relations around the room

The relation view contains eleven entities and fourteen named dependencies. Thermal and evidence paths follow this room’s model and source record. Permission responses enter the reviewed operating plan. Maintenance and shared care describe institutional context; they do not add simulated rooms, ecological scores or live occupants.

Diagram positions organise the dependencies for reading. Fine leaders point to their constructed spatial setting; the diagram is not a map of distances or a communication network running in real time.

Clinic interpretation

The trial category Environmental Variability Deficit requires all three criteria below. The boundaries are authored for this artwork and have no validated clinical status.

Eligibility also requires 72 valid hours, 85% temperature and conditioning coverage, a complete control audit and a resolvable outdoor daily amplitude. These are project settings.

Daily cycle analysis

The program fits an intercept, a sine and a cosine at a fixed twenty-four-hour period, using capped local-duration weights. The indoor amplitude divided by the outdoor amplitude gives the daily ratio. Fit quality describes that daily curve, not the accuracy of the physical model. Phase difference does not establish causation.

Dependence on assumptions

Nine envelope conductances and nine added ventilation rates produce 81 paired comparisons. Each pair uses the same weather. This tests a range of assumptions without assigning probabilities.

The grid runs when requested.

Validation still to do

The current data are generated. Parameter calibration needs one observed period; independent validation needs another. RMSE and signed bias are planned checks against observed temperature, not completed results. The model currently excludes solar gains, moisture, local airflow, microbial relations and health effects.

ERC-01 or a physical opening fragment remains a possible extension. It is not presented here as a completed treatment device.
